9What are the stakes for this first UI?

This sets how much polish, accessibility and brand work goes in before the first Mart uses it.

9APilot Mart first

  • GainFastest to a working shop; feedback from real Cashiers early.
  • GainGood-enough polish and accessibility basics (keyboard, readable contrast, labels).
  • CostLikely rework of look, brand and onboarding before selling to other Marts.

9BCommercial product for many Marts

  • GainPolished brand, WCAG 2.2 AA, white-label ready and guided onboarding from day one.
  • CostMuch more design and test effort before the first Mart goes live.
  • CostDecisions made before real-shop feedback may need changing anyway.

9CPilot first, built commercial-ready

  • GainDesign tokens, theme structure and brand slots in place; visual polish added later.
  • GainPilot ships close to 9A's timeline with less rework afterwards.
  • CostSome upfront structure work the pilot itself won't notice.
